Tickbox is a simple, local-first notes and checklists app. Your notes live on your
device and nowhere else: the app declares no network permission at all, so it is
incapable of sending your data anywhere.

Features:

* Text notes and checklists, with conversion between the two
* Checklists done properly: indent levels, automatic numbering, five bullet styles,
  drag-to-reorder, a tidy section for checked-off items, and progress ("3 of 8 done")
  visible right on the note list
* Search, filters, pinning, and colour labels
* Photo attachments with a pinch-zoom viewer
* Extract text from photos — fully on-device OCR (Tesseract); nothing leaves the phone
* Voice dictation through your device's speech recognizer, disclosed before first use
* Note templates and share as text or HTML
* Full backup: export and import everything, including images, as a ZIP you own

The only permission is Camera, for photo attachments, requested only when you use it.

Tickbox is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later.
